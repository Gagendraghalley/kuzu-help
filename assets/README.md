Logo images the app shows, made from the brand pack in `images/logo/`:

- `images/logo_stacked.png` – splash and welcome screens
- `images/logo_mark.png` and `images/logo_wordmark.png` – app bars

The app icons and launch screens are in `ios/Runner/Assets.xcassets/` and
`android/app/src/main/res/` (the Android 8+ icon is drawn from the vectors in `drawable/`).

Put one icon per service category in `icons/categories/`
(plumber.png, electrician.png, carpenter.png, appliance_repair.png, painter.png, mason.png).
