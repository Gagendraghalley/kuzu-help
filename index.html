Kuzu Help

Local help you can trust

Kuzu Help is a mobile app for Bhutan. Find verified plumbers, electricians, carpenters and other skilled workers near you, and book futsal and football grounds, all in one place.

What you can do

Find a service

Search workers by service and dzongkhag, see their prices, ratings and past work, then call, WhatsApp or send a job request.

Book a ground

See sports grounds near you and their free times, and book a slot. Look around without an account.

Offer your services

Join as a worker, list your services and prices, and get more customers.

Why you can trust it

  • Every worker is checked by our team before customers can see them.
  • Only customers who got in touch with a worker can review them.
  • Customers can report a worker, and our team looks into every report.

Your privacy

We collect only what the app needs, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and data from the app at any time.
